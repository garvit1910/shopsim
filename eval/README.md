### Model-implied vs realised

The table above is the **model-implied** mean of each gate probability over the decisions that actually happened. The realised counts from the same run are below, and they do **not** simply agree: at 89 page decisions the realised column is thin, but its misses are one-sided — every realised rate sits above its model value, not scattered around it. The model estimator is primary because it is the one readable at demo scale, which is a choice of estimator and not a licence to call the gap agreement. `calibration.md` works the standard errors through.
